How the crew directory works

Cali Aircrew is a directory, not a broker. Owners and operators contact crew directly and verify each other, the way the industry already works.

  1. 1

    Crew build a profile

    Aircraft, type ratings, hours, home base and open dates.

  2. 2

    Owners search

    By exact aircraft type, area and availability.

  3. 3

    They connect

    Through Cali Aircrew messaging (coming soon). Crew share phone numbers only when they choose.
