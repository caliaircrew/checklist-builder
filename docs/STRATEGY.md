# Cali Aircrew — how the site evolves

Agreed 2026-10-03 (Steve and James). Companion to `docs/ROADMAP.md`, which holds the task list.

## The core idea
Pilots come for the **free tools**, owners come for the **pilots**, and the site earns from the **businesses around them**.
Cali Aircrew stays a **directory, never a broker or job board**.

## Stage 1 — West Coast private beta (now → next few weeks)
- **Goal:** a directory that feels full in one region: ~50–100 real, approved crew profiles from James's network and 10–20 owners/operators.
- **Build:** Find crew, Browse by aircraft, operator profiles, admin page, Account page, Terms and Privacy.
- **Measure:** listed profiles, searches, and how often an owner actually contacts someone.

## Stage 2 — make connections happen (months 1–6)
- Messaging, availability calendar, FAA verification badges, recommended crew for operators, one-tap résumé.
- Currency and expiration tracker with email reminders: the reason pilots come back monthly, not only when job hunting.
- **First revenue:** paid, clearly labeled partner listings (aviation insurance, sim training, flight schools, FBOs). Crew profiles stay free.

## Stage 3 — grow carefully (6–18 months)
- Expand region by region (Southwest, Pacific Northwest, then nationwide) only once each region has enough crew to search.
- Operator plan (monthly): saved searches, alerts for new crew on their types, team accounts, priority contact.
- Content: contract day-rate survey, Crew Lounge blog, checklist and endorsement libraries (search traffic).
- App: offline install (PWA) first; App Store if usage justifies it.

## Stage 4 — own the niches others ignore
- Helicopters (EMS, tours, utility), mechanics, corporate flight attendants.
- A regional contract day-rate index that pilots and operators cite.

## Principles
- **Free for crew:** profiles and tools are never paywalled.
- **Trust first:** review before listing, verification badges, no raw documents shown, identity checks for account recovery.
- **Tools are the hook:** checklists, endorsements and currency tracking keep pilots engaged between jobs.
- **Directory, not broker:** stays out of charter-broker regulation and employer liability.

## Biggest risks
- An empty directory at launch → region-by-region growth through James's network.
- Fake or exaggerated profiles → review and verification before scale.
- Legal exposure → Terms of Use and attorney review before public launch.
- The name: "Cali Aircrew" is strong for the West Coast but may limit national growth; keep the option of a broader name or sub-brands open (no decision needed now).

## Competitive position (BizJetJobs, reviewed 2026-10-03)
Established national job board plus recruiting service. Cali Aircrew's position: a free West Coast directory with the best per-aircraft detail
(hours, type rating, Part 135 SIC/PIC, available now), helicopters as well as airplanes, and free pilot tools. Borrow ideas; never copy content or design.

## What decides success
An owner searching for a **Citation XLS pilot in Southern California** finds three good, available, verified people. Everything else supports that moment.
