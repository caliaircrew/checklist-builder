# US airport codes → city, state
`airports-us.json` maps US airport, heliport and seaplane-base codes (ICAO, FAA/local, IATA, GPS) to "City, ST".
Source: OurAirports (https://ourairports.com/data/), public domain. Refresh: rerun the snippet in CHANGELOG 1.41
against https://raw.githubusercontent.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data/main/airports.csv.
Used by the crew directory to show a pilot's home airport as "KVNY · Van Nuys, CA" and to check codes in the editor.
